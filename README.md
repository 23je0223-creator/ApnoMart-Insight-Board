## KPI summary
- Total Sales: ₹16,261,998.30
- Total Profit: ₹4,419,736.17
- Total Orders: 1,200
- Weighted Profit Margin: 27.18%

## Key insights
1. Furniture is the largest category by sales, contributing 21.6% of revenue.
2. Office Supplies has the highest category margin (34.80%); Electronics has the lowest (18.88%).
3. Corporate customers contribute the highest segment sales (₹4,311,028); Cash is the most frequently used payment method.

## Strategic recommendation
Protect and grow Office Supplies while reviewing Electronics discounting, supplier costs and product mix before allocating additional promotional budget. Maintain a focused Corporate account plan.

## Important scope note
The assignment title requests Q2 2026, but the provided dataset spans 1 May–28 October 2026. This discrepancy is disclosed in the dashboard and insights document. The weighted profit margin is calculated as total profit divided by total sales.
